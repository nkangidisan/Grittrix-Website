Quick Start

Step 1: Auth

Include your secret key in the header of every request.

Authorization: Bearer gx_key_...

Step 2: Create

Create a payment intent to get a link or QR code.

POST /v1/create-payment

Step 3: Webhook

We'll ping your server the second money hits the account.

EVENT: payment.success
