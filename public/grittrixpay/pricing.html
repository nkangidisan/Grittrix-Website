Frequently Asked Questions

When do I get charged the 0.1% fee?

On the Free plan, 0.1% is automatically deducted from each successful transaction before it lands in your wallet.

How do I connect my MoMo number?

During signup, you'll enter your business MTN or Airtel number. We use this to route payments via STK push.

Is there a contract for Pro?

No. Grittrix Pay Pro is month-to-month. You can cancel or downgrade to the Free plan anytime.

How does the webhook work?

For developers, we send a POST request to your server URL the second a payment is confirmed by the network.
