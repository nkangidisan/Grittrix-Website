The Customer Experience

Under 10 seconds. No USSD codes needed.

1

Opens Link

Customer taps the link and sees your business name and the amount to pay.

2

Enters Number

They enter their MTN or Airtel number and tap "Pay Now".

3

STK Push

A PIN prompt appears automatically on their phone. They enter PIN and payment is done.
