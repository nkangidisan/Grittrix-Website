Business Setup Flow

1

Create Account

Sign up as a business. Connect your MTN or Airtel number where you'll receive payments. No complex bank paperwork required.

2

Create Payment Link

Give your link a name, set a fixed amount (or leave it open for custom payments), and click create. Ready in 30 seconds.

3

Share Everywhere

Send your link via WhatsApp, DM it to clients, paste it on your website, or print it as a QR code for your physical shop front.
