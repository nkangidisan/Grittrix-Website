Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2024

Grittrix Pay respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it. We follow Ugandan data protection laws and global best practices.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you create a Grittrix Pay account, we collect: Full name, Email address, Phone number (including MTN/Airtel), Business name, and Password (hashed).

1.2 Transaction Data

For every payment: Amount, Payer phone number, Method (MTN/Airtel), Timestamps, and Status.

What we do NOT collect:

Your mobile money PIN (ever), bank details, or location data beyond country.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information to: Process payments, send notifications, prevent fraud, and comply with Ugandan financial regulations. We do NOT sell your data or use it for advertising.

5. Security

We protect your data with: AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, and Firebase Authentication. Your PIN is never transmitted to us; it is handled entirely by MTN or Airtel on your device.
