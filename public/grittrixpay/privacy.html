Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2024


Grittrix Pay ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it. We follow Ugandan data protection laws and global best practices.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you create a Grittrix Pay account, we collect your name, email address, phone number, and business name.

Transaction Data: For every payment processed, we collect amount, payer phone number, and transaction status.

2. How We Use Data

We use your information to process payments, send notifications, and prevent fraud.
