Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2024

Grittrix Pay respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it. We follow Ugandan data protection laws and global best practices.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

1.1 Account Information

When you create a Grittrix Pay account, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (including your MTN/Airtel number)
  • Business name (for Business accounts)
  • Password (stored as a cryptographic hash — never in plain text)

1.2 Transaction Data

For every payment processed through Grittrix Pay, we collect payment amount, payer phone number, payment method, and transaction status.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA

We use your information to process payments, send notifications, prevent fraud, and comply with regulations.

We do NOT sell your data to third parties or use it for advertising.

3. DATA SHARING

We share data with MTN Uganda, Airtel Uganda, and Firebase (Google) infrastructure only when necessary to provide our service.

6. SECURITY

We use AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. Your mobile money PIN is NEVER transmitted to or stored by Grittrix Pay.
