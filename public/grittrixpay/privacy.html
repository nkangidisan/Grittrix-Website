Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2024

Grittrix Pay respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it. We follow Ugandan data protection laws and global best practices.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

When you create an account, we collect your full name, email, phone number, and business name. For every payment, we record transaction amounts, payer phone numbers, and timestamps.

We do NOT collect your mobile money PIN. This is entered directly on your phone and handled by your network provider.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA

We use your information to process payments, send notifications, prevent fraud, and comply with Ugandan financial regulations.
